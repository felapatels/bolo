# "You speak it. They answer in English." — the father campaign

CHROME_BIN="/Applications/Google Chrome.app/Contents/MacOS/Google Chrome" \

## The device

**The parent's line is in full colour, in its own script. The child's reply is
flat, grey and in English.** The picture makes the argument before anyone reads
a word. Asset `e-answer-back` inverts exactly that treatment and nothing else,
which is the whole payoff.

Keep that rule if you make more. The moment the child's bubble gets colour for
any reason other than the payoff, the device stops working.

## The maker's mark

**Every asset in this set signs off "Made by a father of ABCDs."** It rides inside
the shared footer lockup, so it is on all seven from one place in the generator.

That signature is the campaign's real weapon, especially next to campaign 1: a
dad versus a company worth billions. Keep it.

**One thing to know about the term.** ABCD splits opinion. Plenty of second-gen
desis find "confused" condescending when it is aimed at them. It works here
because it comes from a father applying it to his own kids, which is the one
position that carries the standing to say it. If it ever appears in a voice that
is not his, it stops being affectionate and starts being a punch down.

## Tone

Not the Duo campaign's swagger and not the AI campaign's confidence. **Warm,
recognisable, a little rueful, never scolding.** The parent in this campaign is
not a failure and the kid is not a villain. Nobody did anything wrong; the
language just needs a way back. Copy that blames either side will not be shared
by either side.

## Where it points

This campaign sells the **Family plan**, which is a **web tier**, so
**TryBolo.app is the real call to action here**, not the store badges. One bill,
up to 4 people, each person's progress stays their own.

**No price appears on any asset, on purpose.** Prices are served from Stripe at
runtime so a displayed price can never drift from the real charge, and a number
burned into a PNG cannot be corrected. Put the price in the caption if you want
it, where you can edit it.
