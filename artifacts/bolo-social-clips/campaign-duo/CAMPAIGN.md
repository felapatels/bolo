## The fact this campaign stands on

**Duolingo teaches exactly one of India's 22 Eighth Schedule languages to
English speakers: Hindi.** Their April 2025 India expansion added Hindi,
Bengali, Tamil and Telugu as **interface** languages, so a Tamil speaker can
now learn Spanish. It taught nobody Tamil. Verified 2026-08-22.

**So the number is 1 vs 22, and Hindi is never in the "forgot" series.** The
Hindi card says something different on purpose. Put Hindi in the forgot pile
and the most-shared asset in the campaign becomes trivially falsifiable.

**Two rules when writing more of these:**

1. **Say "Duo" or "Duolingo", never draw them.** No owl, no wordmark, no
   Duolingo green. Truthful comparison is lawful; borrowing trade dress is
   what draws a letter.
2. **Never widen the claim.** "Duo teaches 1 of 22" is true. "Duo has no
   Indian languages" is false and hands them the correction.
