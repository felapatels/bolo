STORY 1: Hero and positioning
As a heritage learner landing on the site, I want to instantly understand that BOLO teaches me to SPEAK my family's language, so that I know this is not another tap-the-tiles app.
Business value: The hero is the conversion moment; speaking-first is the differentiator and the repositioning widens the addressable audience to the full South Asian diaspora.
Acceptance criteria:
1. The system shall present a hero headline positioning BOLO as speaking-first for South Asian languages (direction: "Actually speak your family's language." with the 22-language breadth as supporting copy, final wording per implementation with owner sign-off at the gate).
2. The system shall replace every public-surface use of "Indian languages" and "22 official Indian languages" with South Asian framing; the count 22 may remain as "22 languages" where it serves credibility.
3. The system shall keep the "Talk, don't tap" tagline element, repositioned or restyled as needed.
4. The system shall render the canonical Bolo mascot per the canonical-mascot rule (whole-image PNG only, gentle idle motion, reduced-motion static, never mounted twice).
5. The system shall present exactly two primary actions above the fold: Get started free and I have an account / Sign in, with no competing CTAs.
6. The system shall keep the hero fully rendered and interactive within the existing home-load performance budget (re-run the existing qa home-load timing probe; no regression against its current numbers).

Acceptance criteria:

2. The system shall source the language list from the same canonical source the app uses (not a second hardcoded list); if the public page cannot query the database, generate the list at build time from the canonical config and document the sync mechanism.

4. The system shall render each language entry with its native script correctly (no tofu, correct fonts per the app's existing native-text handling).
5. The system shall link each language entry to its per-language route (Story 6) when those exist, else to signup.
