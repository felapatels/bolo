BRIEF: Games hub motion punch-up (web) - make the hub feel ALIVE

CONTEXT
The #967 redesign landed color identities and modest entrance/press motion, and the hub still reads static. The bar is raised: the hub should feel like an arcade you walked into, not a settings page with colors. The GamePreview vignettes (per-game SVG/canvas mini-animations) exist in the codebase and are NOT currently rendered on the hub cards; surfacing them is the centerpiece of this pass. Web only; mobile inherits via a later parity pass. Consult docs/CODEBASE-FACTS.md. Use motion engine vocabulary (springs presets) as the base; new named constants join the shared tuning conventions. No em dashes.

STEP 0 (report before building):
1. Locate the GamePreview vignette components: what each of the 5 renders, whether they loop or play-once, their render cost, and why the hub cards currently show static icon bubbles instead.
2. Report the hub's current entrance/press implementation and constants from #967.
3. Confirm reduced-motion handling paths for everything below.

ITEM 1: Live vignettes on cards (the centerpiece)
1. Each card's icon bubble area is replaced or augmented by its GamePreview vignette, playing a subtle idle loop while the card is visible.
2. Idle loops are slow and quiet (ambient, not clamoring); on hover or press-in the vignette animates at full energy (its real preview behavior).
3. Locked cards: vignette renders in the washed-out treatment and does NOT idle-loop; it plays only on hover, reinforcing look-but-locked.
4. Off-screen cards pause their loops (IntersectionObserver or equivalent); total hub CPU stays modest, report before/after.
5. Reduced motion: vignettes render a static first frame, no loops, no hover play.

ITEM 2: Entrance with drama
1. Cards enter with a pronounced staggered cascade: larger travel (rise + slight scale from ~0.9), springs.bouncy or a new springs.poppy variant with visible overshoot, stagger tuned so the full cascade completes under 700ms.
2. The Bolo header mascot reacts to the hub opening: a single wing-flap or bounce via whole-image transform (canonical PNG rule holds) timed to the cascade start. Once per mount, never looping.
3. Reduced motion: everything appears instantly.

ITEM 3: Press and hover theater
1. Hover: card lifts (shadow deepens, slight scale), its color identity saturates slightly, vignette wakes to full energy.
2. Press: a satisfying squash-and-release on the card (deeper than current whileTap), and the pressed card's color briefly blooms (a radial tint pulse from the press point if cheap, else a border glow).
3. Navigating into a game: the pressed card scales up slightly toward the viewer as the route transitions, so entering a game feels like stepping in. Never delays navigation; reduced motion skips all of it.

ITEM 4: Ambient life (restraint clause)
1. At most ONE ambient element beyond the vignette idles per card (e.g. the difficulty badge does nothing; pick nothing extra if vignettes carry enough life). The hub must not strobe; delight dies when everything moves.
2. Free/All-Access pills, lock icons: static.

Manual on dev: hub at 320/480/640/1280 widths; cascade lands with visible bounce, vignettes idle subtly and wake on hover, locked cards stay quiet until hovered, nothing janks on a mid-tier machine, reduced motion is fully static. Report by name with the CPU before/after and WAIT before commit.
