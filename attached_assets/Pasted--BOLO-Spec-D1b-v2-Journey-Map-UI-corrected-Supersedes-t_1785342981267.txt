## Feature summary

Per-language journey map: each language renders as its themed rail line with 6 zones (categories, in order) and stations within each zone, one per lesson_group in position order, phrase-stage stations first, then sentence-stage stations. Station states come from live Slice 2 unlock data; zone/line accessibility reflects M1 teaser states. Zone 6 is the finale.

## Behavior

1. Map renders from live API data only, never mock data.
2. Stations per zone: all lesson_groups of that (language, category) in position order. Post-C1 a zone may hold 3-12 stations (3-6 phrase, 0-6 sentence); layout must handle the full range.
3. Station states, from Slice 2: locked, unlocked, in_progress, completed (latched, never regresses), tested_out. Completion display derives from persisted state, not recomputation.
4. Tapping an accessible station enters the existing practice flow for that group. Tapping a locked station shows the lock reason, distinguishing entitlement locks from progression locks (Slice 2 precedence), and offers test-out where the progression rules allow it.
5. Teaser states, from M1: for a plan-locked language, the map renders in teaser or exhausted mode per the API's teaser fields. Teaser mode: the 3 teaser phrases' home station is accessible and visibly marked as the free taste; all else locked with upgrade affordance; teaser progress (consumed/limit) visible. Exhausted mode: full map visible, everything locked, upgrade prompt keyed to teaser_exhausted copy. The map is deliberately the paywall's showroom: all 6 zones and their station counts render even when locked.
6. Sentence-stage stations are premium (Plus) content: render them distinctly (Step 0 item 6 proposal) and route their locks through the entitlement lock presentation.
7. Unassigned phrases (unassignedCount > 0) never break rendering; groups render from assigned content.
8. Zone order follows the authoritative category table above; a mismatch with the live DB is a hard stop, not a remap.
9. Performance: one language's map renders without fetching other languages' data.
10. Postcard artwork v1: shared frame component, per-line accent color, landmark slot per zone; where no landmark asset exists, render the frame with the zone name. No generated or improvised artwork. The bird-with-flag composite and landmark art remain the separate artwork effort (#801 lineage).

## Verification

- Rendered and checked for: Gujarati (flagship, largest zones post-C1), one language with 4-station Numbers zones (brx/mni/or), one non-Latin-script language, and one teaser-state language viewed as a Free user.
- State transitions: completing a group in practice updates the map on return; test-out pass unlocks the successor; teaser consumption updates the map's teaser progress.
- Entitlement-vs-progression lock presentation verified against a Free user and a (simulated) Plus user.
- Existing suites at the enumerated baselines; no bolo-mobile changes.

## Acceptance: the system shall

1. The system shall render one themed journey map per language using the embedded line and zone names in positional category order.
2. The system shall render one station per lesson group in position order, phrase-stage before sentence-stage, for zones of 3 to 12 stations.
3. The system shall present station states locked, unlocked, in progress, completed, and tested out from live API data, with completed state never regressing.
4. The system shall route accessible-station taps to the existing practice flow and shall present the applicable lock reason for locked stations, distinguishing entitlement locks from progression locks.
5. The system shall render teaser and exhausted modes for plan-locked languages, marking the teaser station, showing teaser progress, and rendering the full locked map as the upgrade showroom.
6. The system shall render sentence-stage stations distinctly as premium content.
7. The system shall render correctly when unassigned phrases exist.
8. The system shall render the shared postcard frame with zone name where no landmark artwork exists and shall not generate artwork.
9. The system shall keep line, zone, and station names in structured content separate from components.
10. The system shall leave bolo-mobile unchanged.
