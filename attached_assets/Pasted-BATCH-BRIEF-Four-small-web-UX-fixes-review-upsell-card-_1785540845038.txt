ITEM 3: Practice word navigation (prev/next), web
As a learner in a practice session, I want to move to the next or previous phrase manually, so that I am not locked to a single phrase until scoring advances me.
Design decision, already made, implement as stated: navigation is FREE (not attempt-gated). Per-phrase state (attempts, bestScore) is already tracked independently of visit order, and the resume logic keys off bestScore per phrase, not sequence position. If Step 0 reveals a technical conflict with this decision, STOP and report instead of improvising.
Acceptance criteria:
1. The system shall provide previous and next controls within a practice session that move between the session's phrases in order.
2. The system shall preserve each phrase's attempt state and scores regardless of navigation order within the session.
3. The system shall disable or hide the previous control on the first phrase and the next control on the last phrase.
4. The system shall not trigger recording, playback, or scoring as a side effect of navigation.
5. The system shall keep existing auto-advance behavior after a scored attempt unchanged; manual navigation is additive.
Implementation notes: controls must not shift the layout of the recording button or waveform area; keep them visually secondary to the record interaction. ITEM 4: Rail pulse visibility strengthening (web)
As a learner viewing the journey after progress, I want the rail pulse to read clearly as energy moving along the line, so that progress feels alive rather than decorative noise.
Current defect: the pulse renders as small dots that are barely noticeable at normal viewing distance and brightness.
Acceptance criteria:
1. The system shall render the rail pulse as a clearly visible traveling effect along the rail at standard screen brightness, distinguishable in the first second of viewing without searching for it.
2. The system shall keep the pulse subordinate to primary content: it shall not distract from station tap targets or compete with station interactions.
3. The system shall respect reduced-motion preferences by replacing the pulse with a static treatment or omitting it.
4. The system shall use existing motion engine vocabulary and named timing constants; any new constants join the shared motion tuning conventions.
Implementation notes:
- Prefer strengthening the existing mechanism over redesign: larger pulse element or a short glowing segment/comet-tail sweep instead of discrete dots, higher contrast against the rail color, possibly slower travel so the eye can catch it. Test at typical brightness, not a dev monitor at full brightness.
- If dots are kept, they need size, glow, or trail treatment; bare dots at current scale are the defect, not the baseline.
- One pulse traveling the line beats many faint ones; density is part of why it reads as noise.
