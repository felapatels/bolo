=== S1: Scoring honesty mitigations (server, api-server evaluation path) ===
Context, established by diagnosis: STT (gpt-4o-mini-transcribe, lang hint from phrase row) normalizes wrong pronunciations into the target transcript; similarity then scores the fabricated transcript; bands derive from score only (perfect >=93, great 80-92, good 68-79, almost 55-67, retry <55); the high-quality retry fires only on empty/sim<=0.40 and its tie-break keeps the transcript CLOSER to the target. No audio-aware evaluation exists anywhere. The full audio-judge fix is scoring v2 (separate brief, calibrated on the labeled capture set); this item ships the honesty layer now.
1. Dual-pass for scored attempts: run both STT passes (mini + high-quality) on every scored practice attempt, not just weak ones. Record both transcripts on the attempt row.
2. Remove the toward-target tie-break: when passes disagree, the attempt is flagged sttDisagreement=true and the band computation uses the transcript FARTHER from the target (the conservative reading). Never silently prefer the target-matching pass.
3. Honesty cap: when the final transcript equals the normalized target AND the passes agree, the band caps at "great" (score capped at 92). "Perfect" is unreachable until scoring v2 exists to earn it. Feedback copy for capped results owns the uncertainty honestly (e.g. "That sounded spot-on to our ears") without claiming flawlessness; adjust the feedback prompt's rubric accordingly so it stops inventing specific flaws it cannot hear (no more fabricated "polish the ending" coaching: the rubric may encourage practice generally but must not name specific sounds it has no evidence about).
4. Nothing else about scoring changes: thresholds untouched (tuning waits for the capture set per the standing rule), XP/FSRS consume the capped score as-is, the evaluation token signs the same fields plus the two transcripts and the disagreement flag.
5. Tests: capped-at-great when transcript==target (the Namasto regression pin, by construction: a perfect-transcript attempt can no longer band perfect), disagreement uses the farther transcript, non-matching transcripts band normally through the existing ladder, token carries the new fields.
Note: the three TTS-cache tests and the pilot-capture env fence must remain untouched and green/fenced as documented.
