Teach Bolo how your alphabet is written

Bolo can already show learners the shape of each letter. What it does not know is the part you learned in school: which stroke comes first, and which way your hand moves. Only someone who writes the script can give it that.

  1. 1Pick the alphabet you write.
  2. 2You will see one letter at a time, greyed out. Trace over it with your finger, the way you would actually write it.
  3. 3Lift your finger between strokes. The order you draw them in is exactly what we need.
  4. 4At the end, tap Copy and paste it into your reply.

More than one person tracing the same alphabet is better, not wasted. Where you all write a letter the same way, that settles it. Where you differ, that is worth knowing too.
