# The 3D Bolo runtime

**In India: ported from Africa on 2026-09-16** (Africa `20aff816`), at the
owner's ask to add it to the other forks: "a" (land on Africa's main with the
full suites, then port from there). Everything below this section is Africa's
design record and was checked there; the checks made HERE are listed in this
section and nowhere else.

**Status here: switched OFF unless a build sets `EXPO_PUBLIC_BOLO3D=on`.** On,
she appears in chat, lessons, the tailor and the daily gift (home and the end of
a session), as in Africa.

**What differs from Africa, and why:**
- **The gift throws kulhads, not cowries** (`GIFT_TOKEN_3D` in
  `lib/bolo3dGift.ts`): India's Chai glyph (`assets/images/stall/kulhad.png`)
  is a terracotta cup.
- **India's gift is two components**, the connected `DailyGiftCard` and the
  drawn `DailyGiftBox`: the box passes a locked press through
  (`onLockedPress`), the card owns the moment, and the moment's amount is its
  own `MomentFare` (India's receipt lives inside the box). The tier table
  (`TIER_SIZE`, `hasRibbon`) moved out of the box into `lib/giftTiers.ts`, so
  the 2D and 3D boxes read one table.
- **The lesson's phrase lines keep India's `SpokenLine` swell**, with the 3D
  sizes added inside it.
- **The lab's sample line is still Africa's Swahili** ("Karibu"), a dev-only
  lip sync fixture, labelled Swahili where it plays. Owed: an Indian line with
  ElevenLabs timings (REGION, low priority, never shipped).

**Not seen on an India simulator**: the engine and screens are Africa's, seen
there; this fork was checked by typecheck and its full suites.

## See it

On the Mac, with this repo's Metro running with `EXPO_PUBLIC_BOLO3D=on` and a dev
client that includes react-native-webview:

```bash
xcrun simctl openurl booted "bolo-mobile://bolo-lab"
```

The lab (`app/bolo-lab.tsx`) is unlinked and outside the signed-in group, so it
opens with no account. Development builds only, unless a build sets
`EXPO_PUBLIC_BOLO3D_LAB=on`.

## Bringing the real Bolo in

1. Put her file at `artifacts/bolo-mobile/assets/bolo3d/bolo.glb`.
2. `node scripts/bolo3d/audit.mjs artifacts/bolo-mobile/assets/bolo3d/bolo.glb`
   checks the brief's machine-checkable sign-off (names, shape keys, sockets,
   all 50 clips, budgets, joint transforms, root motion). Exit 1 on any FAIL.
3. In `artifacts/bolo-mobile/lib/bolo3d.ts`, point `CHARACTER` at `BOLO_RIG`
   and her file.
4. Delete the stand-in: `standin-ducky.glb`, `rigs/standinDucky.ts`,
   `scripts/bolo3d/prepare-standin.mjs` and `repair-standin-eyes.mjs`.

Nothing that renders a bird changes, because nothing names the stand-in: every
caller speaks the contract's moods, clips, sockets and mouth tracks.

## How it is built

| Piece | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| The contract | `lib/bolo-character/src/contract.ts` | `~/bolo3d-poc/brief.html` as code: 10 bones, 11 shape keys, 4 sockets, **50 clips** (the plan said 48; the brief's tables add to 50), budgets, and `auditGltf` |
| Lip sync | `lib/bolo-character/src/visemes.ts` | ElevenLabs per-character timings to mouth shapes, with the spec's three rules (60 ms minimum hold, shut on silence and at the end, 40 ms cross-fade). Latin with tone marks, Ge'ez, Arabic, Brahmic, Hangul. A timing-free rhythm for OpenAI audio |
| Rig profiles | `lib/bolo-character/src/rig.ts`, `rigs/` | Map a file onto the contract. `BOLO_RIG` is empty on purpose |
| The stage | `lib/bolo-stage/src` | three.js 0.182: loads a model through its profile, plays file clips by name and stand-in motion for the rest, the face layer, wardrobe, drag to turn, tap, Reduce Motion |
| Moments | `lib/bolo-stage/src/gift.ts` | A scene with props played on the stage's own clock (the daily gift): her additive turns, the library clips at cues, and beats back to the app |
| The page | `artifacts/bolo-mobile/assets/bolo3d/stage.html` | Generated by `node lib/bolo-stage/build.mjs` (674 KB, one file). `--check` fails if stale. Never edit by hand |
| The app | `components/bolo3d/Bolo3D.tsx`, `lib/bolo3d.ts` | `<Bolo3D mood clip wear>` with an imperative `play`/`speak`/`face` handle and the 2D `<Mascot>` as the still fallback |

## In the screens

Owner, 2026-09-16, choosing between wiring the screens and a phone build first:
"a" (chat, then lessons, then the store, on the branch).

**One switch, off unless a build asks: `EXPO_PUBLIC_BOLO3D=on`**
(`lib/bolo3dFlag.ts`, no imports on purpose). Off, every screen draws today's 2D
`<Mascot>`, the 3D components are never required, and react-native-webview is
never loaded, so the jest suites and any build made without it see today's app.

**On, nothing 3D loads until a screen with her RENDERS** (ledger X92: the 3D
runtime never rides the launch path). Screens call `bolo3dSurfaces()` while
rendering; the gift card requires its moment on the first tap; the labs' routes
require their screens inside the route. **This line used to say the screens'
module-level requires kept her off the launch path, and it was false**:
expo-router evaluates EVERY route module at launch, and log probes on a cold
reload (2026-09-16) showed chat's require running before anything was tapped,
bringing `surfaces`, `Bolo3D` and react-native-webview with it. After the change
the same probes print nothing at launch, the gift tap prints all three, and
chat, the tailor and the lesson still show her.

| Screen | Component | What she does |
|---|---|---|
| Chat (`(tabs)/chat.tsx`) | `TalkingBolo3D`, a twin of `TalkingMascot` | listens while you hold, thinks, talks with a beak rhythm while the reply plays; pulse ring, mic dot and sound bars kept. **She takes no touches here**: her box IS the hold-to-talk button. "Zone complete" gets `celebrate_big` |
| Lessons (`practice/[id].tsx`) | `LessonBolo3D`, `SummaryBolo3D` | listens while recording, thinks while scoring, then `cheer`, `correct`, `head_tilt` for a missed catch, `tryagain` (encouraging), `nod`, `shrug`; talks while the phrase plays (`coachPlaying`); the voice-amplitude scale wrapper still applies. Summaries: `cheer`, `celebrate_big` for a perfect session, `win`, `encourage`, `think` |
| The tailor (`bazaar/OutfitShop.tsx`) | `OutfitPreview3D` | wears the previewed piece, presents it with `show`, and turns to be inspected; stays where the learner leaves her |
| Home's daily gift (`DailyGiftCard.tsx`) | `GiftMoment` (`components/bolo3d/GiftMoment.tsx`), `GiftBolo3D` | **only after a tap**: a locked box, she pulls at it, finds the padlock and points at the words; an earned box, she tears it open, catches a cowrie and offers it, and the amount lands. See "The daily gift" |

## The daily gift

The owner's second pick, from their 3D brief banked 2026-09-13: "Open the daily
gift. Tries lifting it, notices the lock, points at 'Finish a stop today'. Once
earned, tears the box and catches what pops out." (Picks one and three were chat
and the tailor.)

**A moment on tap, not a bird living on home.** Home is the first screen after
launch and the 3D runtime never rides the launch path (ledger X92), so the gift
row stays today's 2D row and the stage mounts only when the box is tapped, in a
modal over home. It is also the owner's own framing: most screens simple, the
bird spent on a few memorable moments. Off, a tap does exactly what it does
today (the row's wiggle, or the claim).

| The tap lands on | She | The words, when her gesture lands |
|---|---|---|
| A box not earned today | turns to it, bends and pulls (the box tips, the padlock swings), lets go, looks at the lock as it rattles, starts at it (`head_tilt`, `surprised`), turns and points (`point`), beckons (`encourage`) | "Finish a stop today to open it.", at the `point` beat, in a bubble whose tail reaches up to her wing |
| An earned box | waits at it, head cocked, while the claim is on the wire (the box nudges, as the 2D box does); once it is GRANTED: grabs the ribbon, yanks, the lid flips off, the sides fall open, one token per unit drawn bursts out, and she catches one in her beak (`correct`), turns and offers it (`give`) | the card's own fare panel, larger, at the `caught` beat: the amount, the sum both ways, tomorrow's line |
| A claim that fails | the box stays, still asking, and she shrugs | "Your gift could not be claimed. Please try again." |
| A box opened today on another device | no box, a nod | "Today's gift is open. Come back tomorrow." |

**Built from the contract's clips, not new ones.** The brief's table says a
strain and a catch are not in the library, and adding names changes the
commission. The scene plays library one-shots at its cues and layers only what
no clip can know as ADDITIVE turns: which way the box is (her whole body turns),
the bend to it, the tremble of a pull, the beak. So it plays over the stand-in's
motion today and over Bolo's authored clips tomorrow.

**Played on the stage's clock.** Her pull and the box tipping are one event;
sent across the bridge as separate commands they would drift apart. The app
sends `moment` commands and hears `point`, `opened`, `caught` and `end` back.
`gift-open` after `gift-waiting` keeps the same box, so the box the learner
watched nudging is the one that opens, and only once the gift was really granted.

**What pops out is the fork's own currency** (`GiftToken`, set per fork as
`GIFT_TOKEN_3D` in `lib/bolo3dGift.ts`, REGION): found porting to Southeast Asia,
where cowrie shells would have paid out a Kopi gift. Each is that fork's 2D
glyph in three dimensions, opened and compared on 2026-09-16 (every fork's file
is called `kulhad.png`, whatever it draws):

| Token | Forks | Built as |
|---|---|---|
| `cowrie` | Africa | a speckled shell with its slit, landing on its back |
| `kulhad` | India, Southeast Asia, Europe | a flared terracotta cup with two ridges, landing on its foot |
| `tea-bowl` | East Asia | white porcelain, a blue rim, amber tea |
| `cacao-cup` | Latin America | a round clay cup, a dark band, foam on top |

A cup hangs from her bill by its rim; a shell sits across it. All four were
photographed headless in flight, held and landed, and under Reduce Motion.

**The box is the 2D box in three dimensions:** indigo body, teal band,
overhanging lid, "Day N" on its front, sized by tier (60, 66, 72, 80) with the
bow on the grand box only, so the tiers still differ by size and shape rather
than hue. The padlock is sized from the grand box at least: on the Day 1 box it
was a gold speck on the simulator.

**Her beak is a measured point now** (`model.beak`, the most forward part of the
head and a hinged jaw, riding the Head joint). The cowrie flies to wherever her
beak IS on the frame it lands, whatever clip is moving it. The feeding game needs
the same point.

**Reduce Motion:** no pull and no flight. The scene jumps to how it ends and
every beat arrives at once, so the words are never held back by motion the
learner asked not to see. If the stage never reports ready, or dies, the words
come anyway: `GiftMoment` counts a fallback from when she is up.

## Touch, on by default everywhere

Owner, 2026-09-16: "i want Bolo 3D to be interactive if someone touches it, it
reacts and can spin it around and look at it, even in lesson screens."

- **A tap reacts to the part it lands on**, read from the skin under the finger
  (a raycast against the posed mesh, then the heaviest bone's contract joint):
  head `head_tilt` / `nod` / `surprised`, beak `peck` / `shake_head`, wing
  `wave` / `shrug` / `clap`, body `fluff` / `correct` / `ruffle`, leg
  `scratch` / `stretch`, rotated so a second tap gets a different answer.
- **A touch never cuts across a clip the screen asked for** (a lesson's
  `correct`); it can cut across its own reactions and the ambient idle glance.
- **Drag sideways to spin her**, with inertia; spun hard, she ruffles once she
  stops; four seconds after the last touch she turns back to the learner
  (`returnToFront={false}` for a dressing room).
- **Vertical swipes scroll the screen under her** (`touch-action: pan-y`), so a
  bird on a lesson page never traps the scroll.

Verified on the simulator: wing to wave, body to fluff, head to nod, leg to
scratch, a hard spin to ruffle, and a swipe that started on her scrolled the
lab.

## Decisions, all reversible

- **The phone renders in a WebView, not with expo-gl.** A GPU or WebGL fault
  kills the WebView's process, not the app, which matters in an app whose
  history is launch crashes from native modules. The same page renders on the
  web, and the model is a plain file or URL, so the real Bolo can arrive without
  a store build once it is served remotely. A native GL host could replace the
  WebView behind the same component. The one native addition is
  `react-native-webview` 13.15.0, SDK 54's pinned version.
- **Every contract clip has a stand-in motion** (`procedural.ts`), so screens
  can use any of the 50 names today. A clip in the loaded file always wins.
- **The lab is a root route with no data.** It exists to be looked at.
- **Work is on a worktree** (`~/bolo-africa-3d`), so sessions on `~/bolo-africa`
  were not disturbed.

## What the stand-in needed, and why each fix is general

"Ducky the duck" (BlueMesh, CC-BY-4.0) arrived at 11.2 MB with three 4096 px
maps, a static fuzz shell, and an export that misplaced things. Each repair is a
general tool the real file may also need:

- **Budget**: `prepare-standin.mjs` dropped the shell, the metal-roughness map
  and tangents, downscaled the maps, and repacked per accessor: 10.7 MB to
  **1.33 MB**, 50,698 to **26,140 triangles**. Repacking per bufferView kept the
  dropped mesh's bytes (4.11 MB): Sketchfab shares one bufferView across meshes.
- **Eyes**: `repair-standin-eyes.mjs` seats each eyeball in the painted ring it
  belongs in, found by UV on the body mesh. They exported three times too large,
  fused, above the head.
- **Joint origins nowhere near the joints**: the head bone sits 1.7 units above
  the head. `pivots: 'skin'` recovers each pivot from where its skin weights hand
  over to its parent. The wing roots and jaw still needed `pivotOverrides`,
  measured in close-up tests: the wing bone owns half the flank, and the lip
  bones smear the jaw's handover along the bill (the bill see-sawed, rendering
  "closed" more open than "AA").
- **A jaw split across siblings**: `jointFollowers` turns the lower-lip chain
  with the jaw, or the bill tears along its edge.

## Traps paid for

1. **A simulator build with `CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO` shows a blank white app
   on iOS 26.5.** It has no keychain entitlement, so Clerk's `saveToken`
   rethrows, Clerk never finishes loading, and `<ClerkLoaded>` renders nothing.
   The tell is expo-notifications' "Keychain access failed: A required
   entitlement isn't present." Build with `CODE_SIGN_IDENTITY=-
   CODE_SIGNING_REQUIRED=YES CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=YES` instead.
2. **`fetch()` cannot read `file://` in either WebView.** The stage reads the
   model with XHR, and the WebView gets `allowFileAccessFromFileURLs` plus
   `allowingReadAccessToURL` set to the directory the page and model share.
3. **`opaque` is not a react-native-webview prop.** A transparent background
   style is what makes the stage see-through.
4. **A proportion fixed for Bolo is wrong for anything else.** Close-up framing
   and wardrobe sizes are measured from the loaded bird (head box, neck
   outline), never hard-coded.
5. **The first stand-in bandana was a flat triangle on the chest**, and the
   owner said so: "bandana doesn't look right", "should wrap the neck". Anything
   worn AROUND a part is built from that part's measured outline and tied to its
   joint (`model.neckRing`, `wardrobe.ts`).
6. **Metro on 8084 can be gone while the app still looks alive.** On
   2026-09-16 it was gone once with no cause established, and once the system
   killed it for low memory. The dev client keeps the bundle it already loaded,
   so screens keep working, but edits stop arriving (the screens lab showed the
   old code) and the next reload fails with "There was a problem loading the
   project". Run `curl -s localhost:8084/status` before trusting a screen after
   an edit, and start Metro again if it says nothing.
